The core

The intelligence core.

The load-bearing idea: built once, called by every door. This is what makes Cadence a platform rather than three tools — and what makes it AI-native rather than a decision-tree bot.

Agentic engine

Understands open-ended intent and acts on it. It routes a message to the right journey, reasons over the client's knowledge (RAG), and calls tools — book a slot, take a payment, fetch a document — over the client's connectors. Low confidence or sensitive intent escalates to a human. This is the difference from the flow-based bots in the reference case studies.

Analytics & metering

Every conversation is measured and metered. The same numbers the reference deployments quote — click-through, read rate, conversion, deflection — surface in the client's dashboard. Usage counts feed billing directly, so revenue tracks activity in real time rather than waiting on an annual contract.

Sentinel

Trust sits under everything.

Not a feature of one door — the floor beneath all of them. Every message passes the gate before the engine acts. The capability incumbents reserve for enterprise, given to every client by default.

Fraud & scam screening

Real-time screening of inbound and outbound messages — phishing, smishing, impersonation — so a small client's verified channel stays safe and their customers stay protected.

Consent & compliance

Opt-in state tracked and auditable; message policy and India's DPDP obligations enforced at the gate. Compliance becomes a property of the platform, not the client's problem.

Working · one conversation

How a single message moves through Cadence.

A patient messages a dental clinic's WhatsApp number. What happens next, end to end.

Step 01 · Arrive
Inbound over WhatsApp

The patient sends a free-text message — "can I move my Tuesday cleaning to Friday morning?"

Step 02 · Gate
Sentinel clears it

The message is screened for fraud and consent state before anything else runs. Only then does it reach the engine.

Step 03 · Understand
The agentic engine reads intent

It recognises a reschedule — not a booking, not an enquiry — and selects the book / reschedule journey. No button tree required.

Step 04 · Act
It calls the clinic's systems

Reads the calendar for Friday slots, offers them in-thread, takes the deposit via the clinic's payment link, and writes the new appointment back.

Step 05 · Confirm & escalate
Human-in-the-loop where needed

The patient gets confirmation. Anything low-confidence or sensitive hands off to clinic staff rather than guessing.

Step 06 · Measure
Metered and logged

The conversation is counted for billing and its outcome recorded — feeding the dashboard the clinic sees.

Working · going live

How a client comes onboard.

The same platform, three speeds — depending on the door.

Self-serve (Console)

Live in a day. Sign up → connect a WhatsApp number → pick a template or vertical pack → connect data via webhook → brand it, set escalation rules → publish.

Managed (Console, done-for-you)

We run it. The same steps, operated on the client's behalf — the entry motion for small clients who don't want to touch the builder. First revenue and first case studies come from here.

Embedded (API)

Keys → sandbox → ship. The builder integrates the API, tests against sandbox, and goes live inside their own product.
