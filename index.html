Telecom · Cybersecurity · SIEM Platform
Aegis
AI-Driven SIEM — DNS intelligence, 6-model threat detection, autonomous mitigation in <60 seconds, forensic console for law enforcement
The Problem
Telecom operators are the first line of defence against cyberattacks, fraud, and criminal activity on their networks — every DNS query traverses their infrastructure. Yet most rely on legacy SIEM tools that generate thousands of undifferentiated alerts, require manual correlation by scarce analysts, and provide no structured mechanism for investigators to search subscriber DNS history. Aegis provides the full stack: passive DNS intelligence, six AI detection models, autonomous threat mitigation, and a court-admissible forensic investigation console — built specifically for the telecom operator context and compliant with CERT-In, TRAI, and DPDP Act 2023 requirements.
